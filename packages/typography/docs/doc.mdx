# Text

## Basic usage
<Playground>
  <Box p={10}>
    <Header>Hello, World</Header>
    <Header tag="h5" weight="thin" color="accent">Here is a subheader</Header>
    <Paragraph>The quick brown fox jumped over the lazy dog.</Paragraph>
    <Small>The quick brown fox jumped over the lazy dog.</Small>
    <Paragraph>H<sub>2</sub>O is one of the most important substances on earth</Paragraph>
    <Paragraph>2<sup>2</sup> = 4</Paragraph>
  </Box>
</Playground>

Text is colored based on the theme context, here is the same text applied within the
'b' variant:

<Playground>
  <Box variant="b" p={10}>
    <Header>Hello, World</Header>
    <Header tag="h5" weight="thin" color="accent">Here is a subheader</Header>
    <Paragraph>The quick brown fox jumped over the lazy dog.</Paragraph>
    <Small>The quick brown fox jumped over the lazy dog.</Small>
  </Box>
</Playground>
