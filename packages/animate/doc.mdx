# Animate
## Basic Usage

This component will animate its direct children.
<Playground>
  <Animate>
    <div>This is a direct child</div>
    <p>
      I'm inside a paragraph tag and I'm a direct child of Animate component
    </p>
    <div>Test</div>
  </Animate>
</Playground>

Images should be wrapped in a container like div
<Playground>
  <Animate>
    <div>
      <Img mr={2} src="https://placeimg.com/640/480/arch" alt='sample' />
    </div>
    <div>
      <Img mr={2} src="https://placeimg.com/640/480/nature" alt='sample2' />
    </div>
    <div>
      <Img mr={2} src="https://placeimg.com/400/1000/tech" alt='sample3' />
    </div>
  </Animate>
</Playground>
